Flourish & Fly Math Diagnostic

Flourish & Fly is a math diagnostic tool designed for K–8 classrooms. Teachers can build a class roster, assign grade and math levels, and track student progress across core math domains. Students log in with a personalized ID and complete adaptive assessments that help identify strengths and areas for growth. Results are displayed in easy-to-read reporting grids so teachers can make informed instructional decisions.
